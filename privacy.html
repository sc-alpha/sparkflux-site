SparkFlux

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Overview

SparkFlux is an educational iOS app. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it. SparkFlux is designed to keep learning activity on your device and does not use cross-app tracking.

Information stored on your device

SparkFlux stores information needed to provide the learning experience, including lesson progress, scores, earned coins, streaks, review history, preferences, and purchase entitlement status. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple platform storage.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored learning progress and consumable balances unless they are otherwise preserved by the operating system. A verified non-consumable Full Course Unlock can be restored through Apple’s App Store services.

Analytics

SparkFlux may maintain simple feature-use counters locally on your device to improve the in-app experience. These local counters are not transmitted to the developer.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. SparkFlux receives transaction and entitlement information needed to verify purchases and provide purchased features. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and terms.

Notifications

If you grant permission, SparkFlux may schedule local reminders on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings. SparkFlux does not require push-notification registration to provide these local reminders.

Tracking, advertising, and third parties

SparkFlux does not track you across apps or websites and does not sell personal information. The current version does not include third-party advertising or remote analytics services.

Children’s privacy

SparkFlux does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because learning information remains on the device and no account is required, the developer does not receive a child’s learning profile through the app. A parent or guardian with questions may contact us using the address below.

Data choices and deletion

You can disable notifications in iOS Settings. You can remove locally stored SparkFlux information by deleting the app. For questions about privacy or data handling, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when SparkFlux features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Email privacy or support questions to sparkflux.support@protonmail.com.
