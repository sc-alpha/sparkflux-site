Support

Help with learning progress, purchases, and app behavior.

Contact support

Email sparkflux.support@protonmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, SparkFlux app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Restore Full Course Unlock

  1. Open SparkFlux using the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Open the Learning Shop or use the purchase controls on the Path screen.
  3. Select Restore Purchases.
  4. Follow any App Store prompts.

Consumable items aren’t offered in SparkFlux version 1.0. The Full Course Unlock is a non-consumable purchase and can be restored through the App Store.

If a purchase is unavailable

  • Confirm the device is connected to the internet.
  • Confirm you are signed in to the App Store.
  • Wait a few minutes and try again.
  • If the issue continues, contact support and include the exact message shown.

Progress or display problems

  • Close and reopen SparkFlux.
  • Install the latest SparkFlux and iOS updates.
  • Check that sufficient device storage is available.
  • Before deleting the app, remember that locally stored learning progress may be removed.

Notifications

Notification permission is optional. Manage it under Settings → Notifications → SparkFlux.
